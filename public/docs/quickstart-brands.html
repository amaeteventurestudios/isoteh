Brand quickstart

Ask a question

Find a space that fits, book it, upload your ad and pay. It goes live when the owner approves it.

  1. Make an account

    Sign up and pick Brand.

  2. Find space

    Open the marketplace and filter by kind of place, topic and country. Each listing shows the audience, the price and the dates still free.

  3. Book and upload

    Pick dates and upload your ad: an image (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 1.5 MB) or plain text with a title, a line and a button. See ad rules and sizes.

  4. Pay

    Pay by card through Stripe, or by bank transfer. The money is held, not paid to the owner yet.

  5. Wait for approval

    The owner approves the ad, and we check it. If the owner says no, your money comes back.

  6. Count the results

    Views and clicks show in Reports by ad and by day. Add the events tag to count sales too.

Want to reach many owners at once instead of booking one space? Start a campaign and pay per 1,000 views.
