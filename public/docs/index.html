Introduction

Ask a question

Isoteh is one ad network for blogs, newsletters, apps, chat and games. Owners sell the space they already have, brands book it directly, and nobody reading is tracked.

#Who these docs are for

OwnersYou run a site, a newsletter, an app, a game or a channel and want to sell ad space.BrandsYou want your ad inside the pages, inboxes and games your customers already use.

#Owners

  • Owner quickstart: list a space and paste two lines.
  • Integration guide: the whole process, from picking a spot to getting paid.
  • Websites and blogs, newsletters, apps and chat, games and creators.
  • Getting paid: when money is held, when it moves, and how to withdraw it.

#Brands

  • Brand quickstart: find a space, book it and upload your ad.
  • Campaigns: pay per 1,000 views across every owner who opts in.
  • Pixel and Conversions API: count sales from your ads without tracking anyone.
  • Ad rules and sizes: what an ad can be, and what is never allowed.

#More

  • How it works, how views count and the API reference.
  • SDKs for websites, apps, chat and games.
  • Changelog and docs for AI tools.
