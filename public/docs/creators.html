Creators

Ask a question

Sell sponsored posts on your own channel. Brands book and pay first, you post with a tracked link, and you are paid three days later.

  1. Add a creator space

    Add your channel as a place and a Creator post space with a price per post.

  2. Share your booking page

    Put it in your bio or media kit.

    URL
    https://isoteh.amaete.com/book/sl_your_slot

  3. Accept a booking

    Read the brief and accept or decline. The brand has already paid.

  4. Post with your link

    Each booking gets its own short link. Use it in the post, then add the post's address in the dashboard.

  5. Get paid

    Three days after you add the post link, or as soon as the brand confirms, 85% moves to your balance.

#If something goes wrong

Either side can open a dispute from the booking. Our team looks at the post and the brief, then pays the creator or returns the money to the brand as credit.
