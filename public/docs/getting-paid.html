Getting paid

Ask a question

The brand pays when they book. We hold the money while the ad runs, then 85% moves to your balance, ready to withdraw.

#When money moves

Kind of bookingMoves to your balance
Banner, native card, game boardWhen the booking's last day has ended
NewsletterWhen the booked issues have been sent
Creator postThree days after you add the post link, or when the brand confirms

#Fees

Listing is free. There is no monthly fee and no setup fee. Isoteh keeps 15% of each booking; you keep 85%.

#Withdraw

Ask for a payout from Money in the dashboard, in US dollars to your bank or PayPal. Payouts are sent by our team today and marked Sent in your dashboard; automatic payouts are on the way.

#Taxes

You are responsible for taxes on what you earn. Keep your payout details and country up to date in Settings.

#Disputes and refunds

If a brand disputes a booking, the money stays held until our team decides. We pay the owner if the ad ran as booked and refund the brand, as credit, if it didn't.
