How it works

Ask a question

A brand pays first, the owner approves, the ad runs where it fits, and the owner is paid once it has run.

#The four rules

  • The owner decides. They set the price for a day, a week or a post, and approve every ad before it runs.
  • The brand pays up front. Money is held while the ad runs and only then paid out. Owners never chase an invoice.
  • The ad, not the person. Views and clicks are counted. Nothing about the reader is stored: no cookies, no profiles, no IP addresses.
  • Always labelled. Every ad carries the word Sponsored, and ads never appear in private chats.

#Two ways to buy

Direct bookingCampaign
How it is pricedThe owner's price for a day, a week or a postA price per 1,000 views you set
Where it runsOne space you pickedEvery space whose owner opted in, matched by topic and country
Who approvesThe owner, before it runsIsoteh reviews the ad; owners choose to join the network
When money movesHeld at booking, paid to the owner after it ranBudget held at start, charged per minute as views come in, the rest returned

#What the owner pastes

Each kind of place has its own small piece of code: a script line for websites, an HTML block for newsletters, a function for apps, a billboard for games and a booking page for creators. The dashboard gives you the right one for each space. See the integration guide.

#What happens to one ad

Each ad comes with a signed token. The view link and the click link carry that token, so we can count each view and click once without knowing who saw it. See how views count.
