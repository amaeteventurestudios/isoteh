Owner quickstart

Ask a question

List a space, paste two lines, and your first booking can arrive the same day.

  1. Make an account

    Sign up and pick Owner. Listing is free and there is no traffic minimum.

  2. Add your place

    In the dashboard, open Places and add your site, newsletter, app, game or channel. Give it a few topic words, like coffee, austin, recipes, so fitting brands find it.

  3. Add a space and set its price

    Pick the kind of space and its size, then a price per day, per week or per post. Turn on Approve each ad if you want to see every ad first (it is on by default).

  4. Paste the code

    Copy the code shown under the space and paste it where the ad should appear.

    HTML
    <div data-isoteh="sl_your_slot"></div>
<script async src="https://isoteh.amaete.com/s/isoteh.js"></script>

  5. Share your booking page

    Every space has its own page at https://isoteh.amaete.com/book/sl_your_slot. Link to it from an Advertise with us page. Your space also appears in the marketplace.

Until a brand books, your space can show your own house ad, an Advertise here card that links to your booking page, or nothing at all. Pick it in the space's settings.
