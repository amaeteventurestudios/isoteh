Core concepts

Ask a question

The handful of words used across Isoteh, in the order you meet them.

#Places and spaces

A place is something you own with an audience: a website, a newsletter, an app, a game or a creator channel. A space (called a slot in the code and the API) is one spot inside it where an ad can go: a banner, a native card, a newsletter block, a billboard in a game or a sponsored post. Every space has an id that starts with sl_.

Kind of spaceWhere it livesPriced by
BannerA website or appDay or week
Native cardA feed, a channel or under an AI answer, drawn in your own styleDay or week
Email blockA newsletter issueIssue or week
Game boardA surface inside a game worldDay or week
Creator postA post on your own channelPost

#Bookings

A booking is a brand buying one space for set dates. Dates can't be sold twice. An unpaid booking holds its dates for 30 minutes, then lets them go.

#Campaigns

A campaign is a budget a brand spends per 1,000 views across many spaces at once. Campaigns hold ad groups, which hold ads. They only show on spaces whose owners opted into the network.

#Views, clicks and events

  • A view counts once when at least half the ad has been on screen for a second. See how views count.
  • A click counts once and sends the reader on to the brand's page.
  • An event is what happened after a click, like a sign-up or a purchase, sent by the brand. See Pixel and Conversions API.

#Money

All amounts are kept in whole cents with a currency code, so 50000 USD is $500.00. Isoteh keeps 15% of each booking; the owner keeps 85%. See getting paid.
