#1. Who we are and who can use Isoteh

Isoteh is run by Amaete Venture Studios ("we", "us"). You must be at least 18 and able to make a binding contract to open an account. If you use Isoteh for a business, you agree to these terms for that business. Keep your sign-in details safe; you are responsible for what happens under your account.

#2. Words we use

  • Owner: someone who lists ad space in a website, newsletter, app, game or channel.
  • Brand: someone who books space or runs campaigns.
  • Space: one spot where an ad can appear.
  • Booking: a brand buying a space for set dates or posts.
  • Readers: the people who see ads in an owner's place.

#3. Using the service

We give you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use Isoteh, its dashboard, SDKs and API while you have an account and follow these terms. You may not: copy or resell the service; get around limits or security; send automated or fake views, clicks or events; scrape the marketplace; or use Isoteh for anything unlawful or in our brand safety rules.

#4. Integration

Owners must install our code as described in the docs, keep the Sponsored label visible, never place ads in private chats, and never count views that did not meet the view rule. We may slow or stop serving to a space that breaks these rules.

#5. Owners

  • You set your prices and may approve or decline any ad.
  • You confirm you have the right to sell the space and that your place follows the law and our rules.
  • You are responsible for any notices your readers need about the ads on your place.

#6. Brands

  • You are responsible for your ads and the pages they link to: they must be true, lawful and yours to use.
  • Ads are images or plain text. We review every ad and may refuse any.
  • If you send events, you confirm you may share them and that they contain no personal data.

#7. Money

  • Brands pay when they book. We hold the money until the booking has run, then pay the owner the price less our fee, which is 15% unless agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Campaign budgets are held when a campaign starts, charged as views are counted, and any rest is returned to the brand's balance.
  • Payouts are sent to the account the owner gives us. Owners are responsible for their own taxes. We may ask for tax forms before paying out.
  • If views or clicks turn out to be invalid, we may adjust, hold or reverse the amounts involved.
  • Brand balances are credit for use on Isoteh and are not a bank account.

#8. Disputes between owners and brands

Either side can open a dispute on a booking. We look at what was booked and what ran, then pay the owner or return the money to the brand as credit. Our decision on the held money is final for that booking.

#9. Data and privacy

We do not track readers. How we handle the information we do keep is in our privacy policy and data processing terms. Numbers about ads (views, clicks, events) may be used to run, improve and protect the service.

#10. Your content and ours

#11. Confidentiality

#15. Indemnity

#16. Suspension and ending
