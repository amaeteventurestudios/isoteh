About Isoteh

Advertising on your terms. We are building the ad network for the people who own the page.

#Why we exist

People with an audience can't easily sell their own ad space. The big networks decide the price, place ads the owner never saw, follow readers from site to site, and pay late. Newsletter writers, small blogs and game makers are mostly left out, or kept inside one platform's walls.

Isoteh is the other way round. The owner sets the price and approves every ad. The brand pays first. Readers are never tracked. And one network covers websites, newsletters, apps, chat and games, so an owner can sell all of it from one place.

#The name

Isoteh comes from the Yoruba word ìṣọtẹ̀, which means rebellion. We say it ee-shaw-teh. It is a small rebellion against advertising that follows people around.

#What we believe

  • The owner decides. Their space, their price, their yes or no.
  • Measure the ad, not the person. Context is enough to place a good ad.
  • Always honest. Every ad says Sponsored. No ads in private chats. No scripts in ads.
  • Paid on time. Money is held from the moment a brand books, so owners never chase an invoice.

#Who we are

Isoteh is made by Amaete Venture Studios, an independent studio in the United States. It is a young product: we are starting with newsletters, independent sites and browser games, including our own.
