Your privacy choices

What you can do about your data on Isoteh, whether you see ads, click them or have an account.

Last updated 6 November 2026

If you see ads through IsotehDo not sell or share my personal informationGlobal Privacy ControlIf you clicked an adIf you have an accountMore

#If you see ads through Isoteh

There is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing to opt out from: we don't set cookies on you, store your IP address, build a profile or follow you across sites. Ads are chosen by the page's topic.

#Do not sell or share my personal information

We don't sell or share personal information, for money or for cross-context behavioural advertising. That stays true for everyone, without a request. If you'd still like it on record, email support@isoteh.amaete.com.

#Global Privacy Control

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal as a request not to sell or share. Since we never do either, nothing changes in what you see.

#If you clicked an ad

The brand's link carries a reference to the ad and the day, never to you. If the brand uses our events tag, it lives only in that browser tab and ends when you close it.

#If you have an account

  • Change your details in Settings.
  • Download or delete your data, or close your account, by emailing support@isoteh.amaete.com.
  • Turn product emails off from any email we send.
