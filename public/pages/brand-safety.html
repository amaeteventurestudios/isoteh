Brand safety

The rules every ad, every place and every view on Isoteh must follow, so owners, brands and readers can trust each other.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Where these rules applyAds must be honestNever allowedAllowed only with our approvalPlaces must be realViews must be realAI answers and chatReport an adWhat we do

#Where these rules apply

To every ad booked or run on Isoteh, every place and space listed, and every view, click and event sent to us. They sit alongside our terms.

#Ads must be honest

  • Clearly labelled Sponsored, and never made to look like the page, the answer or a message from the owner.
  • True, with any important conditions stated.
  • Images or plain text only: no scripts, no HTML, no trackers.
  • Linking to a working page that matches the ad.

#Never allowed

  • Illegal products or services, weapons and explosives, illegal drugs and drug equipment
  • Hate, harassment, violence or anything that demeans people for who they are
  • Sexual content and adult services
  • Scams, get-rich-quick schemes, fake reviews and misleading health claims
  • Malware, phishing and anything that collects data by deceit
  • Ads aimed at children, or placed on places directed at children
  • Political ads and issue ads, for now

#Allowed only with our approval

  • Alcohol, gambling and betting, and anything else limited by age, only where legal and with an age gate
  • Financial products, crypto and lending
  • Health, medical and pharmacy products
  • Dating

#Places must be real

  • Owners list only places they own or run, with a real audience.
  • No ads in private chats, ever.
  • No ad stacking, hidden spaces, or spaces made only to be loaded by robots.
  • No buying traffic to inflate views.

#Views must be real

Views follow the view rule. Robots are filtered. Faking views, clicks or events, or paying people to make them, ends the account and the money involved is returned to the brand.

#AI answers and chat

  • An ad never changes or appears inside an AI answer; it sits apart from it, labelled.
  • Ads are matched to the topic of the conversation, never to a person.
  • No ads in private or direct conversations.

#Report an ad

Seen an ad that breaks these rules? Email support@isoteh.amaete.com with where you saw it. We look at every report and pull ads that break the rules.

#What we do

We may refuse or pull any ad, pause a space or close an account that breaks these rules. Money for ads pulled before they ran goes back to the brand.
