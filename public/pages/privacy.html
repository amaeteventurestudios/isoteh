Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not track the people who see ads.

Last updated 6 November 2026

1. Readers who see ads2. Visitors who click an ad3. Account holders4. Visitors to our own site5. Why we use it6. Who we share it with7. How long we keep it8. Security9. Your choices and rights10. California11. Moving data between countries12. Children13. Changes14. Contact
The short version. Readers who see an ad through Isoteh get no cookies, no profile and no stored IP address. We keep only what we need to run accounts, count ads and move money.

#1. Readers who see ads

When a page, app or game asks for an ad, it sends the space id, topic words and, sometimes, a country. We use these to pick an ad and then forget them. To count views and clicks once, each ad carries a signed token that names the ad, not the person. To stop abuse, we use a hash of the connection that changes every day and is kept only in memory, never written down.

We do not set cookies on readers, store IP addresses, build profiles, follow people across sites, sell or share personal data, or use device ids or fingerprints.

#2. Visitors who click an ad

A click adds a reference to the brand's link. It names the ad and the day, the same for everyone who clicked that ad that day. If the brand uses our events tag, it keeps that reference in the browser tab only (session storage) and sends us events like a purchase with that reference. It sends nothing for visitors who didn't come from an ad.

#3. Account holders

For owners and brands we keep: name, email, password (hashed), organization, team members, places, spaces, ads, bookings, balances and payout details, support messages, and a record of sign-ins for security. Payment cards are handled by Stripe; we never see full card numbers.

#4. Visitors to our own site

Our pages use one cookie, only after you sign in, to keep you signed in. Our pages load their typeface from Google Fonts, which receives your connection's address when it sends the font.

#6. Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need, listed on our sub-processors page, and authorities when the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

#7. How long we keep it

Account data for as long as the account is open and then as long as tax and accounting law requires. Ad numbers are kept as daily totals with no personal data. Server logs are kept for a short time to fix problems.

#8. Security

See our security page.

#9. Your choices and rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your account data, and object to or limit how we use it. Email support@isoteh.amaete.com. We honour Global Privacy Control signals. See your privacy choices.

#10. California

California residents have the right to know, delete and correct their personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not discriminate against anyone for using their rights.

#11. Moving data between countries

We run Isoteh in the United States. Where the law requires it, we use standard contractual clauses for data from the EU, the UK and other places with similar rules.

#12. Children

Isoteh is not for children under 13, and accounts are for adults. We do not knowingly collect children's data. Owners must not use Isoteh on places directed at children without telling us first.

#13. Changes

We will post changes here and tell account holders about important ones.
