Data processing terms

How personal data is handled between Isoteh and the owners and brands who use it. These terms form part of our terms of service; no signature is needed.

Last updated 6 November 2026

#1. Roles

For account data (the people in your organization) and for running the service, Amaete Venture Studios acts as an independent controller. Isoteh is built so that readers' personal data is not needed: we don't receive it in ad requests and we ask brands not to send it in events. Where we process personal data on your behalf at your written request, we act as your processor under section 9.

#2. Words used

"Personal data", "controller", "processor" and "data subject" have the meanings in the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and include "personal information" under US state privacy laws.

#3. What you agree to

  • Not to send us readers' personal data: no names, emails, phone numbers, ids or IP addresses in topic words, events or ads.
  • To have the rights and give the notices your own readers need.
  • To keep your API keys secret.

#4. What we agree to

  • To follow the data protection laws that apply to us.
  • Not to sell or share personal data, and not to use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • To keep personal data confidential and secure (see section 11).

#5. Requests from people

We help each other answer requests from people using their privacy rights, without delay.

#6. Security incidents

We tell affected customers without undue delay, and within 72 hours where we can, after learning of a breach affecting their personal data, and help them meet their own duties.

#7. Sub-processors

We use the providers on our sub-processors page, bound by written terms at least as protective as these. We give 15 days' notice of a new sub-processor on that page; you may object for a good reason.

#8. Moving data between countries

Data is processed in the United States. For transfers from the EU, the EEA, Switzerland or the UK, the standard contractual clauses (and the UK addendum) apply and are made part of these terms.

#9. When we are your processor

We process only on your documented instructions, make sure our people are bound to confidentiality, help with impact assessments, delete or return the data at the end, and give you the information needed to show we meet these terms.

#10. Keeping and deleting

We keep personal data only as long as section 7 of our privacy policy says, then delete it.

#11. Security measures

#12. Order of documents

If these terms and our terms of service disagree about personal data, these terms win.
