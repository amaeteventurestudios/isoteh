Questions.

When do I get paid?

The brand pays when they book. We hold the money while the ad runs, then 85% moves to your balance. Withdraw it to your bank or PayPal in US dollars.

What does it cost to list my space?

Nothing. No monthly fee and no setup fee. Isoteh keeps 15% of each booking, and you keep the rest.

Is there a traffic minimum?

No. A blog with a few hundred readers can list today. You set the price, and brands decide if it fits.

Who decides which ads show?

You do. Every ad waits for your approval. Say no and the brand gets their money back.

Does it work on Substack, beehiiv or Ghost?

Yes. On Substack you paste a ready-made ad block into the post. On tools that allow code, the ad drops in by itself. Clicks count either way.

Which games can use it?

Browser games built with Three.js today, like our own Agent Grand Prix. More engines are coming. A view counts only when the board is really on screen and big enough to read.

How do you count a view?

A view counts when half the ad is on screen for one second. Clicks are counted exactly. Nothing is stored about the person who saw it.

Do you track my readers?

No. No cookies on your readers, no profiles, and no stored IP addresses. Ads are matched to the page and its topic, not to the person.

How do brands see sales without tracking?

A click adds a short code to the brand’s link that names the ad and the day, never the person. The brand’s site or server sends back a sale with that code, and we count it against the ad.

Will ads show in private chats?

Never. Ads appear in public channels and under AI answers only, and always carry the Sponsored label.

What kinds of ads are allowed?

Images and plain text only. No scripts, no pop-ups, no autoplay sound. Every ad is checked before it can run.

How do I start?

Make a free account, list your space, and paste two lines where the ad should sit. WordPress has a plugin. Brands can book from the marketplace right away.
