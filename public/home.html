fair questions.

Will brands book without tracking?

They book your audience and your topic. A barbecue brand wants a barbecue blog’s readers. Views are counted honestly; clicks are exact.

Do I need a big audience?

No. There is no traffic minimum. A newsletter with a few hundred readers can list today and set its own price.

Does it work on Substack?

Yes. You paste a ready-made ad block into the post, and clicks are still counted.

Will ads show up in private chats?

Never. In apps and chat, ads appear in public channels only, and always carry the Sponsored label.
