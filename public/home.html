Questions.

When do I get paid?

The brand pays when they book. We hold it while the ad runs, then 85% moves to your balance. Withdraw it to your bank or PayPal in US dollars.

Does it work on Substack?

Yes. On Substack you paste a ready-made ad block into the post. On tools that allow code, the ad drops in by itself. Clicks count either way.

Which games can use it?

Browser games built with Three.js today, with more engines to come. A view counts only when the board is really on screen and big enough to read.

Will ads show in private chats?

Never. Ads appear in public channels only, and always carry the Sponsored label.
